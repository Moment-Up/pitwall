Navigation: four areas instead of ten tabs

On iPad landscape and the Mac, a 64 px left rail; under 768 px wide, a bottom tab bar. Each area has a sub-nav (pills). The driver chip, connection pill and SYNTHETIC badge are always visible. While the radio is live, a mini radio bar follows you into every area. Old links redirect.

Phone: bottom tab bar

On a phone, Drive becomes a portrait "pit wall" for whoever is watching, and Review becomes a single column. The Session Summary is shown as a beginner sees it: one "Work on" card, plain words, and "?" glossary buttons.

Visual system (dark tokens)

Each colour has exactly one job. Timing colours always come with a tag (PB / SB), and the delta always comes with its sign and the words AHEAD / BEHIND. Contrast ratios are measured against the #050608 background.
