Pitwall UI · Direction B · “Pit wall” (dense)

SAMPLE DATAThis static mockup goes with docs/ui/proposal-pit-wall.md. The circuit (“Halden Ring”), the corner names, the times and the second driver are made up, and nothing here comes from a real session. It is an original design with no series, team or broadcast branding.

Use the controls to step through the Drive states, switch between Pit wall and Focus, and try the light theme or Night dim. Annotations number the panels, and the list under each frame explains them.

sample data · fictional circuit, drivers and times
  1. Status slot (fixed 56 px). Shows the session line, or a flag band: yellow, safety car, red, pit (cyan), no signal (amber outline) or ended. It never pushes content down.
  2. Delta to reference at 144 px with two decimals. Neutral within ±0.02 s. The bar is a number line from −1 to +1 s, labelled AHEAD and BEHIND so the meaning does not depend on colour. Unknown shows -.
  3. The corner that matters now. NOW if inside a corner segment, otherwise NEXT with the distance. The official name comes from track_model.corner_text and is never invented. Focus corners get an orange outline.
  4. Next braking cue from cues.next, with a countdown to the braking point and the last cue’s outcome (the same rule as today). Shows MUTED for a safety car or radio off.
  5. Timing. The running lap shows tenths. Sector cells are coloured as set: purple = household record (REC), green = personal best (PB), yellow = slower. Live S1/S2 times need backend addition D1. LAST, BEST and REC (with the holder’s avatar) sit at 36 px.
  6. Track map. Sectors are coloured by timing status, with your car in orange, the next corner labelled, dashed rings on focus corners, red braking zones, a yellow overlay for flagged zones, and the latest race-control line.
  7. Tyres and energy on a calm scale: in the temperature window = plain white, cold = blue, hot = amber, overheating = red. Wear % and psi, then fuel and ERS.
  8. Inputs. Throttle, brake and speed over the last 12 s (uPlot in the app). Speed and gear sit here rather than in the driver column because the game’s own HUD shows them.
  9. Laps, newest five, coloured by current standing (only the column bests are green or purple). INV, PIT and R (reference) tags; best sectors combined in the header.
  10. Coaching, last lap. The backend’s observation text, shown verbatim with its impact. Positive observations are tagged GOOD. The UI never writes advice.
  11. Rail. Four areas at 64 × 64 px. With Drive lock on track, the other items dim and leaving takes a half-second press.
  12. Top bar. Sub-tabs (44 px), the session line, and the connection, radio and driver pills, which are global on every page.

2 · Navigation: four areas instead of nine tabs

On iPad and desktop the areas sit in a left rail with sub-tabs in the top bar. On phone they move to a bottom tab bar, and Drive always uses the Focus layout. Every old URL redirects, so #/radio keeps working from the iPad home screen.
