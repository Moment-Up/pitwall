Pitwall UI: final design

Superseded (v1, base A). See docs/ui/mockup.html for v2. Clickable mockup for docs/ui/design-spec-v1-base-A.md. The iPad frame is 1180 × 820 CSS px, the Drive baseline. Use the rail inside the frame to move between Drive · Review · Setup · Settings, and the controls below to switch device, theme, driver, data tier and Drive state. The live lap runs on a fictional circuit.

SAMPLE DATA ONLY: a fictional circuit (Kestrel Park), sample drivers and invented lap times. Nothing is connected to the Pitwall API.
SAMPLE DATA

Drive (iPad on the rig)

  • One focal number: the delta (172 px) with a word and a bar that fills left or right. It is neutral inside ±0.02 s.
  • Next corner: the official name appears only where the verified sheet has one (T2, T4 and the other numbered-only corners show the label alone).
  • Cue: a kind chip and an emptying bar, with no flash. The footer shows how the last call landed.
  • On track the rail dims. With Sam (beginner) in the seat, Drive lock asks for a 0.5 s hold to leave Dash.

Timing colours

  • Purple REC: household record (needs D2, so it is never shown with "Today's API").
  • Green PB / SB: your best ever / your best today. Yellow: slower.
  • Switch the data tier to see the honest fallback: last lap's sectors under a caption, and no purple.

Review and Setup

  • Review › Sessions → open a session: Summary, Laps & compare, Corners. The number of Work on cards follows the viewed driver's skill level.
  • Review › Coaching and Progress use the "faster ↑" axis. Setup › Checklist is the pre-flight page.
  • Pages with a dashed note show an existing view that phase 1 wraps unchanged.
